🎫 Support Tickets
Tickets sent from OmniPOS installations (Help → Omni AI → Support Ticket)

Turn the support desk ON when someone is available to answer. While it is OFF (the default), OmniPOS installations keep new tickets on the device and send nothing to RELAY. Installations learn the desk state the next time they refresh from RELAY (at startup, about every 30 minutes, or when the store admin opens the ticket list). Replies you save here are fetched by an installation only when something has changed for it, even if the desk is closed by then.
