Remote BIR test wipe (testing only) →

A client cannot reset its BIR records (System Hard Reset while BIR records exist, or the AGT reset) without a one-time pass key signed here. The client's reset dialog shows a pairing code and appears in Waiting reset requests below by itself: review the details, press Send pass key, and the client's final reset button unlocks automatically. The key is bound to that installation, device, action and request, expires, and works once. A client that cannot reach RELAY can still use the manual request-code flow at the bottom.
