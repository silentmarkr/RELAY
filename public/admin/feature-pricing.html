OmniPOS Unlock Relay — pricing for Pro Themes & Modules

This is where the price/name of every Pro Theme and Module (everything in the catalog except Cloud Backup — that has its own separate subscription pricing page) lives. OmniPOS installations fetch this from GET /relay/pricing on their own (with a local fallback cache in case this RELAY is unreachable), so a change here is picked up by every installation automatically — no redeploy needed on either side. The Upgrade Bundles section below works the same way for the Basic/Standard/Pro bundle prices themselves.
