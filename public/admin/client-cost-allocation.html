📊 Client Cost Allocation
Actual Neon usage, split proportionally per client

How the Neon bill is split fairly across clients.

The Cloud Backup Neon project is shared across every client — Neon only sends one bill for the whole project. This page splits that single bill fairly: each client's storage cost is weighted by their share of total stored data (size_bytes), and their compute cost is weighted by their share of total sync activity (sync_count) — so a bigger, busier store pays more of the real Neon cost, and a small quiet one pays less. The rounded shares always add back up to the exact total Neon cost. A flat maintenance fee (your own markup for monitoring/support — not a Neon charge) is added on top per client. The default is set on the Cloud Backup Pricing page; it can still be overridden individually below. On a client's first successful Cloud Backup subscribe/renew, this fee is marked Paid and excluded from their total for the rest of that billing period — it reappears in their total once that period expires without a renewal. Each card also tracks restore count (last 30 days), an all-time total for context, and last restore time — restores are capped at 5/day and charged a small proportional token fee automatically. The ⚠️ flag triggers at 3+ restores within the last 30 days (a rolling window, not a lifetime count — so an occasional legitimate restore years apart won't stay flagged forever); if a client still looks abusive you can use the ⚡ Charge button to manually deduct extra tokens from their wallet.
