📊 Client Cost Allocation
Actual Neon usage, split proportionally per client

The Cloud Backup Neon project is shared across every client — Neon only sends one bill for the whole project. This page splits that single bill fairly: each client's storage cost is weighted by their share of total stored data (size_bytes), and their compute cost is weighted by their share of total sync activity (sync_count) — so a bigger, busier store pays more of the real Neon cost, and a small quiet one pays less. The rounded shares always add back up to the exact total Neon cost. A flat maintenance fee (your own markup for monitoring/support — not a Neon charge) is added on top per client, and can be overridden individually below.
